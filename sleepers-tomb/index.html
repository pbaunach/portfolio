Windows

For a Windows PC (laptop or desktop)

SleepersTomb-Windows.zip 42 MB

Download for Windows

To play

  1. Right-click the downloaded SleepersTomb-Windows.zip, choose Extract All, then click Extract.
  2. Open SleepersTomb.exe in the folder that appears. There is nothing to install.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This shows up because the demo is not code-signed.

64-bit Windows.

Mac

For a MacBook, iMac or Mac mini

SleepersTomb-Mac.zip 64 MB

Download for Mac

To play

  1. Open the downloaded SleepersTomb-Mac.zip (double-click it) to get Sleeper’s Tomb, then move it to your Applications folder.
  2. The first time, right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose Open, then click Open again. A normal double-click is blocked because the demo is not notarized by Apple.
  3. If macOS says the app is “damaged”, open Terminal, type xattr -cr followed by a space, drag the app onto the Terminal window, and press Return. Then open it again.

Apple silicon (macOS 11 or later) and Intel (macOS 10.13 or later).
